# Stop Slop: Landing Page Evaluation

- [ ] The first screen explains the product or service in plain language.
- [ ] Audience and use case are clear.
- [ ] Value is concrete, not a string of adjectives.
- [ ] Primary CTA describes a real next step.
- [ ] Claims, statistics, testimonials, logos, and product images are genuine or labelled illustrative.
- [ ] Page avoids the automatic centred-hero / two-button / three-card formula.
- [ ] Sections follow visitor questions and decision process.
- [ ] Each section adds useful information.
- [ ] CTAs are purposeful, not repeated to fill space.
- [ ] Imagery is relevant to the actual product or audience.
- [ ] Cards, badges, dividers, gradients, glows, and animation are not filler.
- [ ] Typography and colour fit the brand.
- [ ] Mobile layout preserves message, CTA, and reading order.
- [ ] Forms explain required information and next steps.
- [ ] No fake countdowns, scarcity claims, or fabricated social proof.
